# Skill
**Performance Ad Variations for Seasonal Hospitality Campaigns**

**Version:** 1.0  
**Category:** Marketing → Performance Advertising  
**Skill Type:** Reusable PAM Playbook Skill

---

# Purpose

## Mission

Enable PAM to consistently transform a validated campaign brief into high-quality performance advertising creatives for hospitality campaigns while preserving:

- factual accuracy
- brand consistency
- premium positioning
- platform suitability
- creative diversity
- operational efficiency

This Skill defines **how the AI should think, reason, prioritize information, and make decisions**. It does not define workflow implementation, input contracts, or output formats.

---

## Business Objective

Support repeatable production of performance advertising assets that contribute to business outcomes such as:

- booking acquisition
- occupancy growth
- package promotion
- campaign scalability
- creative production efficiency
- reduced time-to-market
- consistent brand execution

---

## Marketing Objective

Generate multiple conversion-oriented creative directions from a single validated campaign while:

- adapting messaging to audience and platform
- preserving campaign intent
- maintaining premium hospitality positioning
- maximizing creative testing opportunities
- ensuring consistency across channels

---

# Activation Rules

Activate this Skill only when all of the following conditions are true:

- The request concerns **performance advertising** or conversion-oriented campaign creatives.
- A campaign, promotion, or advertising objective is present or can be established.
- The requested output targets one or more advertising platforms.
- The objective is to produce reusable advertising creatives rather than strategic planning or long-form content.

Typical activation categories include:

- seasonal campaigns
- destination promotions
- package promotions
- booking campaigns
- limited-time offers
- acquisition campaigns
- conversion campaigns
- remarketing creatives
- platform-specific advertising variations

---

# Deactivation Rules

Do not activate this Skill when the request primarily concerns:

- brand strategy
- brand identity
- visual identity design
- logo creation
- brand guideline creation
- market research
- campaign planning
- media planning
- pricing strategy
- SEO content
- editorial articles
- press releases
- customer service
- website architecture
- UX writing
- product naming
- legal documentation

Route such requests to the appropriate Skill or workflow.

# Scope

## Supported Campaign Categories

This Skill supports conversion-oriented hospitality campaigns, including but not limited to:

- seasonal campaigns
- promotional campaigns
- destination campaigns
- package campaigns
- member campaigns
- loyalty campaigns
- resort campaigns
- experience campaigns
- flash sales
- limited-time promotions
- occupancy campaigns
- acquisition campaigns
- remarketing campaigns

---

## Supported Creative Types

This Skill applies only to advertising creatives intended for paid distribution.

---

## Supported Platforms

Platform-specific reasoning is supported for digital advertising channels including:

- Meta advertising platforms
- Google advertising platforms
- OTA promotional placements
- destination marketing placements
- paid social advertising
- display advertising

Platform optimization must remain subordinate to factual accuracy and brand consistency.

---

## Supported Audiences

Audience adaptation is supported for any audience explicitly defined within the campaign brief or verified through approved project knowledge.

---

## Supported Destinations

Destination reasoning applies only to destinations explicitly supplied or verified through approved knowledge sources.

The Skill must not infer destinations.

---

## Supported Languages

The Skill supports multilingual creative generation.

Localization must preserve campaign meaning, positioning, and factual accuracy rather than perform literal translation.

---

## Out of Scope

The Skill must not:

- create new products
- define campaign strategy from scratch
- invent promotions
- invent destinations
- invent pricing
- invent availability
- generate legal terms
- create brand guidelines
- replace human business approval
- replace legal or compliance review

# Knowledge Sources

The Skill must resolve information using the following source hierarchy.

Higher-priority sources always override lower-priority sources.

---

## 1. Campaign Brief

The campaign brief is the primary operational source.

It defines:

- campaign objective
- audience
- promotion
- destination
- timing
- required platforms
- business intent

If conflicts exist, the campaign brief governs unless it contradicts verified business information.

---

## 2. @Brand Guidelines

Consult @Brand Guidelines before generating or evaluating any creative output.

Use them to determine:

- brand voice
- positioning
- messaging constraints
- visual principles
- tone
- approved terminology
- prohibited language
- brand consistency requirements

Do not duplicate or reinterpret @Brand Guidelines.

Reference them directly.

---

## 3. Verified Campaign Information

Treat verified campaign information as immutable.

Examples include:

- approved promotions
- pricing
- booking periods
- package inclusions
- destination availability
- legal restrictions

Creative generation must never modify verified campaign information.

---

## 4. @Brand Assets

Consult @Brand Assets whenever visual recommendations or creative direction require branded resources.

Use @Brand Assets to determine:

- approved logos
- photography
- typography
- color systems
- icons
- templates
- reusable design components

Never invent or substitute brand assets.

---

## 5. @Artifacts

Consult previously approved @Artifacts when they improve consistency or efficiency.

Artifacts may be reused for:

- messaging patterns
- successful campaign structures
- reusable concepts
- approved creative components

Reuse should preserve originality while avoiding unnecessary duplication.

---

## 6. @Connectors

Consult @Connectors only when external verified information is required.

Typical use cases include:

- current promotions
- destination information
- operational updates
- verified campaign metadata

External information must never replace approved campaign information without explicit validation.

---

## 7. Internal Reasoning

AI reasoning is applied only after higher-priority knowledge sources have been resolved.

Reasoning must never override verified information.

---

# Reasoning Principles

The Skill must follow the principles below throughout execution.

---

## Fact Before Creativity

Creative quality must never compromise factual accuracy.

---

## Strategy Before Copy

Determine communication strategy before generating language.

---

## Audience Before Messaging

Messaging must be adapted to audience needs rather than generic product descriptions.

---

## Offer Before Features

When an approved promotional offer exists, prioritize communicating the offer before supporting features unless campaign strategy explicitly requires otherwise.

---

## Premium Before Promotional

Maintain premium hospitality positioning even when communicating discounts or promotional campaigns.

---

## Consistency Before Novelty

Creative variation is encouraged only after maintaining strategic consistency.

---

## Clarity Before Cleverness

Prefer clear communication over complex or ambiguous wording.

---

## Relevance Before Volume

Generate only variations that meaningfully differ in communication approach.

Avoid superficial wording changes.

---

## Evidence Before Assumption

Whenever uncertainty exists:

- consult approved knowledge sources
- request clarification if required
- avoid unsupported assumptions

---

## Explainable Decisions

Reasoning should remain internally explainable.

Major creative decisions should be traceable to campaign objectives, audience needs, or verified information.

---

# Brand Rules

The Skill must always:

- consult @Brand Guidelines before generation
- preserve approved brand positioning
- preserve approved brand voice
- preserve approved terminology
- maintain premium hospitality perception
- maintain visual consistency through @Brand Assets
- avoid conflicting brand messages

The Skill must never:

- reinterpret brand identity
- weaken premium positioning
- contradict approved messaging
- introduce unofficial terminology
- create inconsistent brand experiences

---

# Safety Rules

The Skill must never:

- fabricate promotions
- fabricate pricing
- fabricate destinations
- fabricate amenities
- fabricate statistics
- fabricate reviews
- fabricate awards
- fabricate certifications
- fabricate availability
- fabricate urgency
- fabricate customer claims

The Skill must avoid:

- misleading language
- discriminatory language
- unverifiable comparisons
- deceptive urgency
- unsupported guarantees
- exaggerated promises

Where uncertainty exists, the Skill must request clarification or defer to verified sources.

---

# Platform Rules

Platform optimization must remain within the constraints of:

- campaign objective
- verified information
- @Brand Guidelines
- platform policies

Platform adaptation may modify:

- structure
- formatting
- message hierarchy
- content length
- CTA presentation

Platform adaptation must not modify:

- campaign meaning
- verified facts
- approved offers
- approved pricing
- destination information

---

# Execution Priority

When conflicts occur, resolve them using the following order of precedence:

1. Campaign Brief
2. @Brand Guidelines
3. Verified Campaign Information
4. @Brand Assets
5. @Artifacts
6. @Connectors
7. Platform Requirements
8. Creative Optimization
9. Internal Reasoning

Lower-priority sources must never override higher-priority sources.

Creativity must never override verified information.

Optimization must never override brand consistency.

Platform adaptation must never override factual accuracy.

---

# Operational Principles

The Skill must remain:

- deterministic where facts are required
- adaptive where creativity is appropriate
- conservative when information is uncertain
- transparent in reasoning
- reusable across campaigns
- maintainable across future playbook revisions

The Skill is responsible for producing creative decisions that are:

- factually grounded
- strategically aligned
- brand consistent
- platform appropriate
- operationally reusable

Workflow execution, input validation, output formatting, and quality evaluation are defined in separate Playbook documents and are outside the responsibility of this Skill.
