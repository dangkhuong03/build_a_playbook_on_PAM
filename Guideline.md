## Do & Don't
**Do:**
- Use verified promotions.
- Use official destination names.
- Keep messaging concise.
- Maintain premium brand positioning.
- Focus on customer experience.
- Produce multiple creative variations.
- Preserve brand consistency.

**Don't:**
- Invent promotions.
- Invent pricing.
- Invent facilities.
- Use misleading claims.
- Overload creatives with text.
- Use generic stock photography.
